## Explore the villages of Paros

### Parikia

The island capital and main port mixes a lively waterfront with a maze of whitewashed lanes, boutiques, and the magnificent Byzantine church of Panagia Ekatontapyliani (the Church of 100 Doors). Arrive by ferry, grab a coffee on the waterfront, then lose yourself in the old town before sunset.

### Naoussa

Once a humble fishing village, Naoussa is now the chic heart of Paros: a postcard harbour with a Venetian castle ruin, excellent seafood tavernas, and a buzzing nightlife scene. Come early for swimming nearby, stay late for dinner by the boats.

### Lefkes and the mountain villages

Head inland to Lefkes, the former capital perched in the hills, with marble streets, shady squares, and the historic Byzantine Road walking trail. Nearby Prodromos and Marpissa reward visitors with authentic Cycladic charm and fewer crowds.

## The best beaches in Paros

- **Kolymbithres**, surreal smooth granite rock formations and turquoise coves near Naoussa.
- **Golden Beach (Chrissi Akti)**, a long sandy stretch and a world-famous windsurfing spot.
- **Santa Maria**, shallow, family-friendly water and beach bars.
- **Monastiri**, sheltered swimming inside the Paros Park area.
- **Faragas**, a scenic cove in the south, great for a quieter day.

## Day trip to Antiparos

No visit is complete without the short hop to [Antiparos](/antiparos-island/). Explore the dramatic Cave of Antiparos, relax on uncrowded beaches, and wander the cosy main town. The car ferry from Pounta takes about 7 minutes.

## Food, culture and sunsets

Eat grilled octopus in Naoussa, try local cheeses and wines inland, and chase sunsets from Parikia’s waterfront or the west-facing beaches. For a quieter evening, Antiparos town squares fill with families and soft music after dark.

## Sample 3-day itinerary

1. **Day 1**, Arrive Parikia, old town + Ekatontapyliani, sunset swim.
2. **Day 2**, Naoussa harbour, Kolymbithres, evening dinner by the boats.
3. **Day 3**, Lefkes walk + Antiparos day trip via Pounta.

## Practical tips

- July, August is peak: book cars and rooms early.
- Shoulder months (May, June, September) are ideal for driving and beaches without crowds.
- Parking in Naoussa and Parikia centres can be tight, arrive early or use edge lots.
