## Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to all your questions in our FAQ section! From booking and rental policies to transfer services and accommodation, we’ve got you covered with everything you need to know for a smooth and enjoyable experience.

#### What are the requirements to rent a scooter in Antiparos?

You need a valid driver’s license A1 (motorcycle), usually from your home country, and be over 24 years old. Helmets are mandatory.

#### How much does it cost to rent a scooter in Antiparos?

Rental prices vary depending on the season and duration of rental. Expect prices to range from €20 to €60 per day.

#### What typCan I rent a scooter if I don't have a motorcycle license?es of cars are available in your fleet?

No, you cant rent a motorcycle without a specified driver’s license but you could drive an ATV with a car license.

#### Are scooters easy to park on Antiparos?

Yes, scooters are very convenient for parking in Antiparos due to their compact size. Parking spaces are widely available.

#### Do I need insurance when renting a scooter in Antiparos?

Yes, all rental scooters come with basic insurance coverage. Additional insurance options may be available for extra protection.

#### What should I do in case of scooter breakdown or accident?

Contact our rental office immediately for assistance. We provide roadside support and can arrange for scooter replacement if necessary.

#### Can I return the scooter to a different location on Antiparos?

Generally, scooters are rented and returned to the same location. Contact us for specific arrangements if you need to return the scooter elsewhere.

#### Are there any restrictions on where I can ride the scooter on Antiparos?

You can ride scooters on most roads and paths on Antiparos. However, some areas may have restrictions or require special permits. Always follow local traffic laws.

#### Is it safe to ride scooters on Antiparos?

Yes, Antiparos has relatively low traffic, making it safe and enjoyable to ride scooters. Wear helmets and drive cautiously, especially on narrow roads.

#### Can I rent scooters for a group or event on Antiparos?

Yes, we offer group rentals for events and special occasions. Contact us in advance to discuss your specific needs and availability.
