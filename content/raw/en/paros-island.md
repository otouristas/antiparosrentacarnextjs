Paros is a Cyclades classic: two harbour towns with distinct personalities, inland marble villages, beaches from granite coves to windsurf strands, and effortless links to Antiparos. This destination guide helps you plan a first visit, or a smarter return trip.

![Kolymbithres Beach on Paros with turquoise water](/media/2025/02/Kolimpithres-Beach-Paros.webp)

## Snapshot

- **Main towns:** Parikia (port) and Naoussa (chic harbour) 
- **Airport:** Paros (PAS) 
- **Signature beaches:** Kolymbithres, Golden Beach, Santa Maria, Faragas 
- **Day trip:** Antiparos via Pounta 

## Parikia

Arrive by ferry, explore the old town alleys, visit Panagia Ekatontapyliani, and use the waterfront as your practical base for the first night.

## Naoussa

Come for the fishing harbour, Venetian castle ruins, boutique energy, and some of the island’s best dining. Nearby beaches make it a strong multi-night base.

![Naoussa village harbour at golden hour](/media/2026/06/paros-naoussa-village.png.webp)

## Inland Paros

Lefkes, Prodromos, and Marpissa show a cooler, quieter side of the island, perfect for morning walks and long lunches.

## Beaches worth the drive

Kolymbithres for geology and photos, Golden Beach for sport and sand, Santa Maria for families, and southern coves when you want fewer umbrellas.
