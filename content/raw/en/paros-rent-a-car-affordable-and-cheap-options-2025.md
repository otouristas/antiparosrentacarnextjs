## What drives the price

- **Season**, July, August costs more than May or late September 
- **Vehicle class**, mini vs automatic SUV 
- **Lead time**, last-minute peak weeks are expensive 
- **Extras**, child seats, additional drivers, out-of-hours delivery 

## How to save without cutting corners

1. Book early for peak dates 
2. Choose the smallest car that fits your group and bags 
3. Prefer clear local operators over vague third-party quotes 
4. Avoid “too cheap” listings that push insurance upsells at the counter
