#### Can I pick up my rental car at Paros Airport or Paros Port?

Yes, AGGELOS Rentals offers convenient pick-up and drop-off services at both Paros Airport and Paros Port. Simply select your preferred location during the booking process, and we’ll ensure your car is ready when you arrive.

#### Do you provide rental cars for trips to Antiparos?

Absolutely! Our cars are perfect for exploring both Paros and Antiparos. With our flexible rental options, you can easily travel between the islands and enjoy their beautiful landscapes at your own pace.

#### What types of cars are available in your fleet?

We offer a wide variety of vehicles, including compact cars, SUVs, and luxury models, to suit all preferences and budgets. Whether you’re traveling solo, as a couple, or with family, you’ll find the perfect car for your trip.
