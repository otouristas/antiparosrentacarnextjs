Heading to the Cyclades via **Piraeus**? Sea-festival season and summer ferry peaks turn Greece’s main port into a lively, and busy, gateway. This guide helps you use Piraeus well on the way to Paros and Antiparos, without losing a day to chaos.

## Why Piraeus matters for island trips

Most travellers still reach Paros by ferry from Attica. Whether you are catching a Blue Star overnight or a high-speed day boat, Piraeus is where the journey becomes real, tickets, gates, luggage, and timing.

## Festival & summer energy

Seasonal waterfront events and summer crowds can make the port area festive and full. Enjoy the atmosphere, but treat ferry day as logistics-first: know your gate, arrive early, and keep documents offline.

## Smooth ferry-day checklist

1. Confirm departure terminal / gate the evening before 
2. Arrive with buffer for taxis and security lines 
3. Keep a soft bag easy to carry on crowded decks 
4. Pre-book your **Paros rental** so pickup is waiting in Parikia
