## Fly into Paros (PAS)

Domestic flights from Athens are frequent in summer. From the terminal you can:

- Meet your **AGGELOS Rentals pickup** (pre-booked)
- Take a short taxi into Parikia
- Connect onward to your hotel if you already have a car waiting

Flying saves time in peak season when ferry queues are long, but seats sell out, so book early.

## Ferry to Parikia (Paros port)

Blue Star and high-speed services run from Piraeus; seasonal links also connect Rafina and other islands. Arrive into **Parikia**, the main port town.

Tips for a smooth landing:

- Keep your booking QR/ticket offline
- Expect baggage carts and queues in July, August
- If you have a rental, walk or ride a short transfer to the agreed meeting point, we confirm details by WhatsApp

## Island-hopping from Mykonos, Santorini & Naxos

High-season catamarans and ferries link Paros with neighbouring islands. Schedules change by month, always recheck 48 hours before travel. Build buffer time if you have a same-day rental return.

## Reaching Antiparos

From Paros, drive or bus to **Pounta** for the short car ferry to Antiparos (about 7 minutes). Foot passengers can also use the passenger boat from Parikia in season.
