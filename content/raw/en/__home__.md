#### **Tips for Renting Vehicles in Antiparos and Paros**

When renting a vehicle on these islands, here are a few tips to make your trip stress-free:

1. **Plan Ahead:** Secure your rental early, especially during peak summer months, to guarantee availability.
2. **Choose the Right Vehicle:** Cars are ideal for families and longer distances, while scooters, ATVs, and bicycles are perfect for exploring smaller streets and off-road areas.
3. **Know the Roads:** Both islands have well-maintained roads, but some rural areas may be narrow or unpaved. Our team is happy to guide you on the best routes.
