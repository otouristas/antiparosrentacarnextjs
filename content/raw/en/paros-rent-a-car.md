## Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to all your questions in our FAQ section! From booking and rental policies to transfer services and accommodation, we’ve got you covered with everything you need to know for a smooth and enjoyable experience.

#### What do I need to rent a car in Paros?

You need a valid driver’s license (car) from your country of residence and be over 21 years old. International driving permits are recommended for non-EU residents.

#### How much does it cost to rent a car in Paros?

Rental prices vary by vehicle type, season, and duration. Expect prices to range from €20 to €90 per day for standard cars.

#### Are there any additional fees when renting a car in Paros?

Additional fees may include insurance upgrades, airport pickup/drop-off charges, and fees for extra accessories like GPS navigation systems.

#### Can I rent a car if I'm under 25 years old?

Yes, but drivers under 25 may incur a young driver’s surcharge due to insurance policies. Check with us for specific details and requirements.

#### What insurance options are available when renting a car in Paros?

Our rentals include basic insurance coverage. Additional options, such as collision damage waivers (CDW) and theft protection, are available for enhanced peace of mind.

#### Are there restrictions on where I can drive the rental car in Paros?

You can drive our rental cars on all paved roads in Paros. Off-road driving is generally not permitted unless using an appropriate vehicle like an SUV or ATV.

#### What should I do in case of a car breakdown or accident in Paros?

Contact our rental office immediately for assistance. We provide roadside support and can arrange for a replacement vehicle if needed.

#### Can I return the rental car to a different location in Paros?

Generally, rental cars are returned to the same location. Contact us to discuss any special arrangements for returning the car elsewhere on Paros.

#### Is it safe to drive a rental car in Paros?

Yes, driving in Paros is generally safe. Follow local traffic laws and drive defensively, especially in busy areas and during peak tourist seasons.

#### Can I rent a car for a group or event in Paros?

Yes, we offer group rentals and special rates for events and large gatherings. Contact us in advance to discuss your specific needs and availability.
