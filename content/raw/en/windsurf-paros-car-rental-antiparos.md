Paros is a European windsurf hotspot, reliable summer *meltemi*, long sandy beaches, and schools for every level. This guide covers where to ride, how to get there with gear, and why a rental car (or van-like space) makes the week smoother.

![Windsurfing on the east coast of Paros](/media/2025/07/Windsurf-in-paros.jpg.webp)

## Where to windsurf on Paros

- **Golden Beach (Chrissi Akti)**, the classic long sandy stretch 
- **New Golden Beach**, nearby spots with school infrastructure 
- Neighbouring coves depending on wind direction and level 

Always check local school briefings for daily conditions.

## Getting to the beach with kit

Hotels in Parikia or Naoussa are not “next to” the main windsurf beaches. Without a vehicle you will burn time and money on transfers, especially if you ride morning and afternoon sessions.

> **Session logistics matter.** A compact or crossover car gets you, your board bag (if travelling with gear), and friends to the beach on your schedule.

## Car vs scooter for watersports weeks

| | Car | Scooter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wet gear & bags | Easy | Awkward |
| Group of 3, 4 | Comfortable | No |
| Evening Naoussa dinners | Easy | Fine |
| Peak meltemi roads | Stable | Challenging |

Most windsurf travellers choose a **car**. ATVs are fun for dry beach days but less ideal with serious kit.
