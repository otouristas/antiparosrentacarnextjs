Practical travel tips for **Paros and Antiparos**, packing, timing, money, ferries, and how to avoid the classic first-timer mistakes. Written for 2026 trips with real pickup and rental logistics in mind.

![Paros and Antiparos islands from above](/media/2026/06/paros-antiparos-islands.png.webp)

## 1. Book the “boring” bits early

Flights into PAS, popular ferry seats, and **automatic / family cars** sell out first in July, August. Lock transport and rental before you obsess over tavernas.

[Check rental availability →](#book)

## 2. Pick the right base

- **Parikia** if you arrive by ferry and want convenience 
- **Naoussa** for dining and style 
- **Antiparos town** for quieter nights 

Compare islands: [Paros vs Naxos vs Antiparos](/paros-vs-naxos-vs-antiparos/).

## 3. Pack for wind and sun

The *meltemi* can be strong on east-coast beaches. Bring a light layer, reef-safe sunscreen, water shoes for rocky entries, and a dry bag for boat days.

## 4. Cash still helps

Cards are widely accepted in towns, but small beach canteens and parking machines can be cash-friendly. Keep some euros handy.

## 5. Give yourself ferry buffer

Never schedule a same-day tight connection between a late ferry and an international flight. Build slack, especially with luggage.

![Ferry deck approaching the Cyclades](/media/2026/06/paros-ferry-deck.png.webp)

## 6. Decide car vs scooter honestly

Scooters are fun for couples with light bags. Families, cave trips, and supermarket runs favour a car. Read [Do you need a car in Paros?](/do-you-need-to-rent-a-car-in-paros/).

## 7. Respect village parking

Naoussa and Parikia centres get tight after 19:00. Park once, walk, and enjoy dinner without circling.

## 8. Antiparos day trips are easy, overnight is better

One day covers the cave and a beach. Two nights lets the island’s slower pace sink in.

Golden Beach is a drive from most hotels. If you are taking lessons, a rental keeps you on schedule. See [Windsurf Paros](/windsurf-paros-car-rental-antiparos/).
