Short answer: **for most visitors, yes**, if you want beaches beyond the bus stops, mountain villages, and flexible Antiparos day trips. You can survive without a car in high season if you stay in Parikia or Naoussa and stick to organised tours, but you will miss a lot of Paros.

![Coastal road on Paros with a compact rental car and Aegean views](/media/2026/06/paros-rent-car-road.png.webp)

## When you can skip a car

- You are staying in **Parikia or Naoussa** for 2, 3 nights and mainly want harbour walks, nearby beaches, and nightlife.
- You are happy with **buses** (summer frequency is decent on main corridors) and taxis for evenings.
- You prefer **scooter or ATV** for short hops and accept luggage limits.

## When a car (or larger vehicle) pays off

- Beach-hopping: Kolymbithres, Santa Maria, Golden Beach, Faragas, and quieter south coves.
- Inland villages: Lefkes, Prodromos, Marpissa, and the Byzantine Road.
- Families with kids, beach gear, or multiple bags.
- Day trips that combine Paros mornings with an **Antiparos** afternoon via Pounta.

> **Honest local take:** taxis add up fast in July, August. A compact car for 3, 4 days often costs less than repeated transfers, and you keep your schedule.
