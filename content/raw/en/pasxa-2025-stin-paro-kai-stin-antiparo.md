Greek Orthodox Easter on **Paros and Antiparos** is one of the most atmospheric times to visit the Cyclades, candlelit processions, spring light, quieter beaches, and villages that feel deeply local. Here is how to plan a spring island trip around Easter week.

![Traditional village scene on Paros in spring](/media/2025/03/xorio-paros.webp)

## Why Easter week feels special

- Milder weather than midsummer 
- Cultural celebrations in towns and villages 
- Fewer beach umbrellas, more space to wander 
- Ideal for couples and families who prefer calm over clubs 

Dates shift each year with the Orthodox calendar, confirm the exact weekend before you book ferries.

## Where to stay

**Parikia** and **Naoussa** for services and dinners; **Antiparos town** if you want quieter evenings after the celebrations. Many tavernas open for the holiday even when summer season has not fully started, but hours can vary, so ask locally.

## Getting around in spring

Public buses are thinner outside peak summer. A **rental car** makes village-hopping, evening events, and beach stops far easier, especially with parents, grandparents, or kids in tow.

## Suggested spring itinerary

1. Arrive Paros, settle, sunset walk 
2. Village day (Lefkes / Marpissa) + local church evenings 
3. Antiparos day or overnight 
4. Beach picnic on a calm west- or south-facing cove 

![Antiparos beach in softer seasonal light](/media/2026/06/antiparos-beach.png.webp)

## Practical notes

- Pack layers, evenings can be cool 
- Confirm ferry schedules for shoulder season 
- Reserve rentals and rooms sooner than you think
