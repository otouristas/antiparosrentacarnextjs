Antiparos is the quieter sister of Paros, a compact Cycladic island of sandy beaches, a famous cave, and a charming main town where evenings still feel local. It is perfect for couples, families, and anyone who wants Cyclades beauty without the megaclub volume.

![Aerial view of Antiparos Island](/media/2025/02/Aerial-View-of-Antiparos-Island-1024x576.webp)

## Why visit Antiparos

- Walkable town centre with tavernas and shops 
- Soft beaches within a short drive or scooter hop 
- The Cave of Antiparos, a dramatic underground landmark 
- Easy ferry link to Paros for shopping, flights, and Naoussa nights 

## Getting there

Most visitors reach Antiparos via **Paros**:

1. Ferry or fly into Paros 
2. Transfer to **Pounta** for the ~7-minute car ferry 
3. Or take the seasonal passenger boat from Parikia 

Full arrival detail: [How to get to Paros](/how-to-get-to-paros-ferries-flights-airport/).

## Best things to do

1. **Cave of Antiparos**, go early in summer to avoid heat and queues 
2. **Town & castle area**, wander lanes, swim nearby, stay for dinner 
3. **Beaches**, calm sands and clear water; bring shade in peak months 
4. **Sunset on the west side**, simple, memorable, uncrowded compared with famous neighbours

Many travellers sleep on Antiparos and day-trip to Naoussa, or reverse the plan. The channel crossing is short, treat the two islands as one flexible itinerary.
