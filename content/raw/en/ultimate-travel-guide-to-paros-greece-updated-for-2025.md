## Why Paros

Paros balances Cycladic beauty with real infrastructure: a proper airport, frequent ferries, two lively towns, inland villages, and some of the best watersports beaches in Greece. It is an ideal first Cyclades trip, and a favourite return destination.

## When to visit

| Season | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| **May, June** | Warm seas building, fewer crowds, great driving |
| **July, August** | Peak energy, book cars & rooms early |
| **September** | Warm water, softer nightlife, excellent value |
| **Oct, Apr** | Quieter; check ferry/flight frequency |

## Where to base yourself

- **Parikia**, ferry hub, old town, practical for arrivals
- **Naoussa**, style, dining, nearby beaches
- **East coast / Golden Beach**, windsurf & long sand
- **Inland (Lefkes area)**, cooler evenings, village calm

## Must-do experiences

1. Naoussa harbour at sunset 
2. Kolymbithres rock pools 
3. Lefkes & the Byzantine Road 
4. Day trip to [Antiparos](/antiparos-island/) 
5. Windsurf or watch the action at Golden Beach

## Food & evenings

Expect fresh seafood, grilled meats, local wines, and late dinners. Naoussa leads for nightlife; Antiparos for slower nights under plane trees.

## Suggested week

- Days 1, 2: Parikia arrival + Naoussa 
- Days 3, 4: beaches + Lefkes 
- Day 5: Antiparos 
- Days 6, 7: watersports or west-coast sunsets
