## Explore the Villages of Paros

### Парикия

The island capital and main port, Parikia mixes a lively waterfront with a maze-like old town of whitewashed lanes, boutiques and the magnificent Byzantine church of Panagia Ekatontapyliani (the Church of 100 Doors).

### Наусса

Once a humble fishing village, Naoussa is now the chic heart of Paros: a postcard harbour with a Venetian castle, excellent seafood tavernas and a buzzing nightlife scene.

### Lefkes and the mountain villages

Head inland to Lefkes, the former capital perched in the hills, with marble streets, shady squares and the historic Byzantine Road walking trail. Nearby Prodromos and Marpissa reward visitors with authentic Cycladic charm and few crowds.

## The Best Beaches in Paros

- Kolymbithres, surreal smooth granite rock formations and turquoise coves near Naoussa.
- Golden Beach (Chrissi Akti), a long sandy stretch and a world-famous windsurfing spot.
- Santa Maria, shallow, family-friendly water and beach bars.
- Monastiri, sheltered swimming inside the Paros Park area.
- Faragas, a scenic cove in the south, great for a quieter day.

## Часто задаваемые вопросы

**What is Paros known for?**

Paros is famous for its charming villages like Naoussa and Parikia, beautiful beaches such as Kolymbithres and Golden Beach, world-class windsurfing, fine Parian marble, and a relaxed atmosphere with fewer crowds than Mykonos or Santorini.

**What are the best beaches in Paros?**

Top beaches include Kolymbithres for its rock formations, Golden Beach for windsurfing, Santa Maria for families, Monastiri in Paros Park, and Faragas in the south.

**How many days do you need in Paros?**

Three to four days lets you enjoy the main villages, several beaches and a day trip to Antiparos. A week is ideal if you want to relax and explore at a slower pace.

**Is Paros good for families?**

Yes. Paros has shallow, sheltered beaches like Santa Maria, family-friendly tavernas and easy distances, especially with a rental car for carrying beach gear.

**Do you need a car to explore Paros?**

You can use buses for the main towns, but a rental car is the best way to reach the island's beaches, mountain villages and hidden coves on your own schedule.
